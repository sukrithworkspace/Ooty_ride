Frequently asked questions

Do I pay on this website?

No. This demo prepares the booking and opens WhatsApp. Payment can be discussed after your trip is confirmed.

Can I select multiple places?

Yes. Tap Add to Trip on any destination. Selected destinations are included in the booking.

Is the price estimate final?

No. The estimate is only a guide. Final pricing depends on vehicle, route, days and requirements.

Can I change my travel date?

Yes. Use the Change / Cancel option in the booking window and send the request through WhatsApp.
